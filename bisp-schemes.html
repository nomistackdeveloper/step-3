BISP Phase 2 Distribution Details

The Government of Pakistan has officially released the upgraded quarterly financial assistance of Rs. 13,500 under the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP). Eligible families can check their disbursement status online or through the official SMS verification gateway.

How to Check Your CNIC via 8171:

  1. Open your mobile message inbox.
  2. Type your 13-digit CNIC number without any dashes or spaces.
  3. Send the message to 8171.
  4. You will receive an automated reply confirming your family's eligibility and designated payment collection camp.

Important Safety Guidelines:

Always collect your full stipend amount from authorized biometric ATM franchises (HBL, Bank Alfalah) or designated campsite partner banks. Never pay any fee or deduction to unauthorized agents.
