Online Step-by-Step Guide

How to Apply via PSID (GoPb Portal)

PPSC strictly uses paperless online fee generation. Traditional bank challans are discontinued.

1

Generate PSID Code

Start your online profile submission on the PPSC portal to receive your unique 17-digit Personalized Subscriber ID (PSID).

Paper Challans Not Accepted
2

Deposit Fee (Rs. 600)

Pay Rs. 600 using your PSID via 1Bill / GoPb options on JazzCash, EasyPaisa, UPaisa, Mobile Banking, or ATMs.

JazzCash EasyPaisa 1Bill ATM
3

Upload Scanned Documents

Upload passport photo with light background, CNIC front image, and final BS/Master's Degree transcript. Enter exact marks/percentages.

Passport Photo & Transcript
4

Final Application Submission

Verify all details and click "Submit Application". You will receive an instant confirmation text message and application code.

Save SMS Application No.
