Punjab Police Recruitment Notification 2026

Sponsoring Body: Punjab Police Department, Government of Punjab

The Punjab Police Department has officially announced new vacancies for Constables, Lady Constables, Driver Constables, and Special Protection Unit (SPU) Wireless Operators across all 36 districts of Punjab. Candidates meeting the required educational and physical standards can download the application form and submit it to their district police lines.

Standards for Male Candidates
  • Minimum Height: 5 Feet 7 Inches (170 cm)
  • Chest Size: 33" x 34.5" Inches
  • Running Test: 1.6 KM in 7 Minutes
  • Age Limit: 18 to 22 Years (Extended for Tribal Areas)
Standards for Female Candidates
  • Minimum Height: 5 Feet 2 Inches (157 cm)
  • Chest Size: Not Applicable
  • Running Test: 1.6 KM in 10 Minutes
  • Age Limit: 18 to 22 Years

Step-by-Step Application Submission Process

1

Download Application Form

Download the official job application form from the official Punjab Police website (punjabpolice.gov.pk) or collect it from your district police lines.

2

Attach Attested Documents

Attach attested copies of your Educational Certificates (Matric/Inter), CNIC, Domicile Certificate, Character Certificate, and 8 recent passport-size photographs.

3

Submit at District Police Lines

Submit the filled application along with an application fee (~Rs. 500) to the designated recruitment desk in your respective District Police Lines.

4

Physical & Written Test Slip

Collect your physical measurement test slip and roll number for the 1.6 KM running test schedule.
