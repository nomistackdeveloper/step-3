Privacy Policy

At Govt Jobs & Schemes PK (accessible via easy2find.site), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by our website and how we use it.

1. Log Files and Analytics

easy2find.site follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to easy2find.site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Privacy Policies of Advertising Partners

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons
