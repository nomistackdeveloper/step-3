Overview of the Kisan Card Initiative

The Chief Minister of Punjab has launched the landmark Kisan Card Scheme to provide direct financial relief and agricultural subsidies to registered farmers across all districts of Punjab. Through this card, farmers can purchase certified seeds, DAP fertilizers, and pesticides on easy installment terms without paying any interest.

Key Benefits & Features:

  • Interest-free seasonal loans for purchasing agricultural inputs.
  • Direct subsidies transferred securely via digital banking channels.
  • Special quota reserved for small landowners owning up to 12.5 acres of agricultural land.
  • Transparent verification system linked directly with computerize land revenue records (LRMIS).

How to Apply via SMS:

Type your 13-digit CNIC number (without spaces or dashes) and send it to 8070. You will receive an SMS confirmation regarding your land verification status and eligibility.
