About CM Punjab Kisan Card Ecosystem

Jointly Managed by Dept of Agriculture Punjab, PITB & Bank of Punjab (BOP)

The CM Punjab Kissan Card scheme is a digital financial intervention designed to protect small and medium farmers from high interest rates and unverified market inputs. The initiative has expanded rapidly, providing over Rs. 360 billion in interest-free credit to more than 900,000 registered landholders across Punjab.

Target Scale: 900,000+ Cards Issued
Land Eligibility: 1 to 12.5 Acres
Tenor Cycle: 6 Months + 1 Month Grace[cite: 1]
Usage: Authorized Seeds, DAP & Fertilizer Dealers Only[cite: 1]

Interactive Kisan Card Loan & Landholding Estimator

Enter your PLRA land size (acres) to estimate season credit limits and verify 1 to 12.5 acre rules.

Strict Eligibility Cross-Checks

PLRA Land Record Integration[cite: 1]

Applicant must own between 1 to 12.5 acres of land digitized in the Punjab Land Records Authority database[cite: 1].

PTA SIM Registration Check[cite: 1]

The mobile number used for sending SMS to 8070 must be registered strictly on the applicant's own CNIC[cite: 1].

Clean eCIB Standing

The applicant must have no bank loan defaults or adverse flags in the electronic Credit Information Bureau.

Input-Only Facility[cite: 1]

Credit cannot be withdrawn as cash; it is restricted to buying certified seeds, DAP, and pesticides from registered vendors[cite: 1].

Automated Digital Application Workflow

1

Send SMS Check to 8070

Type PKC <Space> CNIC Number (13 digits without hyphens) and send to 8070 from your registered SIM.

2

NADRA & PLRA Verification

Automated cross-checks verify your land record from PLRA and national identity metrics from NADRA.

3

Biometric Wallet Activation

Upon approval notification, visit an HBL Konnect agent or Bank of Punjab (BOP) branch to complete biometric verification and open a "Kisan Wallet".

4

Physical Card Collection

Pay a one-time card fee (~Rs. 500) and pick up your Kisan Card from your designated Agriculture Tehsil Office or BOP branch.

Farmer Scam Advisory

The official SMS code for Kisan Card registration is strictly 8070. Do not respond to unofficial private numbers or pay cash processing fees to unauthorized persons.

  • Format: PKC <CNIC> sent to 8070.
  • Fees are paid directly at BOP/Agriculture Tehsil offices.
