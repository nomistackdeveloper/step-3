FPSC Inspector FIA & Custom Officer Recruitment Overview

Federal Public Service Commission, Islamabad

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) invites online applications from male and female citizens of Pakistan for gazetted posts of Inspector FIA (BS-16) and Customs Inspector / Preventive Officer (BS-16) under the Federal Revenue Board (FBR) and Ministry of Interior. Recruitment is conducted on a strict merit and provincial quota system.

FIA Inspector Male Standards
  • Minimum Height: 5 Feet 6 Inches (168 cm)
  • Chest Size: 32" x 33.5" Inches
  • Education: Bachelor Degree (2nd Class or Grade C)
  • Age Limit: 20 - 28 + 5 Years General Relaxation = 33 Yrs
FIA Inspector Female Standards
  • Minimum Height: 5 Feet 2 Inches (157 cm)
  • Chest Size: Not Applicable
  • Education: Bachelor Degree (2nd Class or Grade C)
  • Age Limit: 20 - 28 + 5 Years General Relaxation = 33 Yrs

FPSC MCQ Screening Test Pattern & Syllabus

The FPSC screening test consists of a 100 Marks Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) exam divided into two core parts:

Part-I: English Grammar & Vocabulary (20 Marks)

Sentence structuring, prepositions, vocabulary, synonyms, antonyms, and grammar usage.

Part-II: General Ability (80 Marks)

Basic Mathematics/Arithmetic (20m), General Knowledge (20m), Pakistan Affairs & Islamic Studies (20m), Every Day Science (20m).

Step-by-Step FPSC Online Application Process

1

Deposit Treasury Challan Fee

Download the FPSC Treasury Challan Form and deposit Rs. 300 at any branch of National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) or State Bank of Pakistan (SBP).

2

Visit Official FPSC Web Portal

Go to fpsc.gov.pk and click on "Apply Online" for the specific advertisement case number.

3

Fill CNIC & Bank Deposit Details

Enter your CNIC number, Challan deposit date, NBP branch code, and your academic degree marks accurately.

4

Submit Application & Print Slip

Submit your application online. Print the tracking receipt for future admission certificate download.
